02Sample read

What a read looks like

A scan should be a board you can read in one glance. Numbers and flags, then a sentence. This sample is a layout, not a live result.

  • SecurityIs the token safe to buy and sell? Selling blocks, hidden fees, who can change the rules. Checked fresh on every scan.72
  • CommunityAre real people interested? Its own X page and real accounts talking about it. Bots are ignored.41
  • FoundationIs there a real project behind it? Age, website and docs, exchange listings, backers.50

Scores 90 and up show green, 60 and up yellow, 30 and up orange, under 30 red. Overall 80 and up reads low risk, under 40 high risk.
