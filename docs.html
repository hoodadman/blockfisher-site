How the pieces are meant to work

One page for the token scanner extension, the token scanner, fund tracing, and the scanners coming later.

Token scanner extension

The Chrome extension is the main way to use Blockfisher: a plain-English token check on the page where you buy. It watches DexScreener, CoinGecko, jup.ag, Uniswap, GMGN and Birdeye, and on any other site you can click the Blockfisher icon. When the page has a token address, it runs the same scan as the token scanner on this site. On a pair page it scores the base token.

Token scanner

You paste a token’s public address. The read should say, in plain language, whether a sale can be stopped, whether new supply can be created, and whether one owner can change the contract after you buy.

Book an investigation

Have you been caught in a scam? We can help. You say what you signed, what left the wallet, and when. A person follows the trail and tells you what can still be done. Booking is a request for a quote.

Investigations are quoted per request, starting from $500. Large cases are charged as a deposit plus a percentage of funds recovered, agreed in writing before you pay. Investigations do not guarantee results: we cannot promise that funds will be recovered or frozen.

Coming later

These scanners are not for sale yet. Each one launches on its own, only once it is built, tested in house, and tested by the public.

Wallet scanner

You paste a public address. The read should say if that address has touched a drainer, a known scam, or a cluster already tied to stolen funds. You can also list token contracts that wallet deployed, with the contract address and a DexScreener link. It never asks for a seed phrase or a private key.

DeFi app scanner

You give the app’s site or contract. The read should say whether the page you see, the contracts it calls, and the approval on the screen are the same system.

Blockchain scanner

You pick a chain. The read should say if the client, a bridge, or the validator set is showing a fault that is already public. Ethereum, Solana, Base, Arbitrum, Polygon, BNB Chain, and Bitcoin are the first names on the list.

Portfolio scanner

You point it at positions you already hold. The read should say which of them became riskier since the last check, without making you open five tools.

Blockfisher spots the phish before you bite, and follows the trail when you've been caught.
